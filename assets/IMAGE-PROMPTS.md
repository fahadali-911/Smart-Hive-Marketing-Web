# SmartHive Case Study Image Prompts

These project-local images were created with the built-in image-generation tool and compressed to WebP for the website.

## `case-hero.webp`

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: premium med-spa case studies hero image
Primary request: editorial photograph of a sophisticated modern med-spa consultation lounge and treatment environment
Scene/backdrop: warm ivory stone, pale oak, soft curved plaster walls, discreet treatment bed and consultation seating, subtle botanical detail
Style/medium: high-end architectural and wellness editorial photography, photorealistic, natural material texture
Composition/framing: wide landscape composition suitable for a website hero, balanced depth, clear focal area
Lighting/mood: soft diffused daylight, serene, premium, trustworthy, clinically clean without looking like a hospital
Color palette: warm cream, muted sage, deep forest accents, restrained brushed brass
Constraints: realistic interior, original scene, no text, no logos, no watermark, no cartoon styling, no excessive gold, no medical cross, no honeycomb decoration
Avoid: dental clinic appearance, harsh white hospital lighting, cheap spa clichés, people, clutter
```
